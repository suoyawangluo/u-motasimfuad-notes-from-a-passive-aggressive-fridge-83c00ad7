One funny passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its owner, for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-notes-from-a-passive-aggressive-fridge-83c00ad7) bounty. Referral: [xxbm6cca](https://make.fun/r/xxbm6cca).

## The note

> I counted: you opened my door 47 times today and took nothing. At this point, just move in.

The fridge counts every door opening with its sensors and resents the owner for opening it 47 times and taking nothing. The sting lands in the last sentence.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the note to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), verifies the gate (1–200 characters, single line, non-blank) and prints its sha256.
